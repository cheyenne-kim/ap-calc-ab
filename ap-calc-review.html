Review

Focused final review · area/volume, optimization, related rates, implicit diff, motion, and curve analysis
This page is built as a last big pass through the highest-friction AP Calc AB ideas: geometry setup, rate relationships, particle interpretation, and derivative-based reasoning about extrema and concavity. The set is intentionally mixed in difficulty so it feels closer to a real review day than a worksheet of one-note problems.

Best Habit

Set up the quantity before you compute it.

That matters especially for volume, optimization, and related rates.

For Motion

Velocity, acceleration, distance, displacement are different.

Distance uses \(|v|\); speeding up needs \(v\) and \(a\) with the same sign.

For Curve Analysis

Local extrema come from \(f'\); concavity comes from \(f''\).

Keep those tests separate.

Area & Volume

1
EasyArea
Find the area between \(y=x\) and \(y=x^2\) on \([0,1]\).
Answer: A) \(1/6\)
On \([0,1]\), \(x\ge x^2\). Area \(=\int_0^1(x-x^2)\,dx=\left[\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1=\frac12-\frac13=\)\(\frac16\).
2
MediumDisc Method
Find the volume when the region under \(y=2x\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=1\) is revolved about the \(x\)-axis.
Answer: B) \(4\pi/3\)
\(V=\pi\int_0^1(2x)^2\,dx=4\pi\int_0^1x^2\,dx=4\pi\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1=\)\(\frac{4\pi}{3}\).
3
HardWasher Method
Find the volume when the region between \(y=x\) and \(y=0\) on \([0,2]\) is rotated about \(y=2\).
Answer: B) \(16\pi/3\)
Outer radius \(R=2\), inner radius \(r=2-x\).
\(V=\pi\int_0^2\left(4-(2-x)^2\right)dx=\pi\int_0^2(4x-x^2)\,dx\)
\(=\pi\left[2x^2-\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2=\pi\left(8-\frac83\right)=\)\(\frac{16\pi}{3}\).
4
EasyTotal Area
Find the total area enclosed by \(y=4-x^2\) and the \(x\)-axis.
Answer: C) \(32/3\)
Zeros at \(x=\pm2\). Area \(=\int_{-2}^{2}(4-x^2)\,dx=\left[4x-\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_{-2}^{2}=\)\(\frac{32}{3}\).
5
HardVolume
Find the volume when the region between \(y=x^2\) and \(y=2x\) is rotated about the \(x\)-axis.
Answer: B) \(64\pi/15\)
Intersections at \(x=0,2\). Outer radius \(R=2x\), inner radius \(r=x^2\).
\(V=\pi\int_0^2((2x)^2-(x^2)^2)\,dx=\pi\int_0^2(4x^2-x^4)\,dx=\)\(\frac{64\pi}{15}\).

Optimization

6
EasyOptimization
A rectangle has perimeter 20. What is its maximum possible area?
Answer: C) 25
The maximum area rectangle for a fixed perimeter is a square. Side \(=20/4=5\), so area \(=5^2=\)25.
7
MediumFencing
A farmer has 100 meters of fencing for three sides of a rectangle along a river. What dimensions maximize the area?
Answer: B) \(25\) by \(50\)
Let \(x\) be each side perpendicular to the river and \(y\) the side along the river. Then \(2x+y=100\Rightarrow y=100-2x\).
\(A=x(100-2x)\), so \(A'=100-4x=0\Rightarrow x=25\). Then \(y=50\).
8
HardOpen-Top Box
An open-top box with a square base has volume \(108\text{ cm}^3\). What dimensions minimize surface area?
Answer: B) side \(=6\), height \(=3\)
Let base side \(x\), height \(h\). Volume: \(x^2h=108\Rightarrow h=108/x^2\).
Surface area: \(S=x^2+4xh=x^2+432/x\). Then \(S'=2x-432/x^2=0\Rightarrow 2x^3=432\Rightarrow x^3=216\Rightarrow x=6\). Then \(h=108/36=3\).
9
MediumInscribed Rectangle
A rectangle is inscribed under the line \(y=8-2x\) in the first quadrant with sides on the axes. What is the maximum area?
Answer: B) 8
\(A=x(8-2x)=8x-2x^2\). Then \(A'=8-4x=0\Rightarrow x=2\), and \(y=4\). Max area \(=2\cdot4=\)8.
10
HardClosest Point
Which point on the line \(y=2x+3\) is closest to the origin?
Answer: B) \((-6/5,3/5)\)
Minimize distance squared: \(D^2=x^2+(2x+3)^2=5x^2+12x+9\).
\((D^2)'=10x+12=0\Rightarrow x=-6/5\). Then \(y=2(-6/5)+3=3/5\).

Related Rates

11
EasySphere
Air is pumped into a sphere so that its volume increases at \(36\pi\text{ cm}^3/\text{s}\). How fast is the radius increasing when \(r=3\)?
Answer: A) \(1\) cm/s
\(V=\frac43\pi r^3\Rightarrow \frac{dV}{dt}=4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt}\).
At \(r=3\): \(36\pi=36\pi \frac{dr}{dt}\), so \(\frac{dr}{dt}=1\).
12
MediumLadder
A 13-ft ladder leans against a wall. The base slides away at \(5\) ft/s. How fast is the top sliding down when the base is \(5\) ft from the wall?
Answer: A) \(25/12\) ft/s
\(x^2+y^2=169\). Differentiate: \(2x\frac{dx}{dt}+2y\frac{dy}{dt}=0\Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dt}=-\frac{x}{y}\frac{dx}{dt}\).
When \(x=5\), \(y=12\). Thus \(\frac{dy}{dt}=-(5/12)(5)=-25/12\). The top slides down at \(25/12\) ft/s.
13
HardCone
A cone satisfies \(h=2r\). If its volume is increasing at \(6\pi\text{ cm}^3/\text{s}\), find \(\dfrac{dr}{dt}\) when \(r=3\).
Answer: B) \(1/3\)
\(V=\frac13\pi r^2h=\frac13\pi r^2(2r)=\frac23\pi r^3\). Then \(\frac{dV}{dt}=2\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt}\).
At \(r=3\): \(6\pi=18\pi\frac{dr}{dt}\), so \(\frac{dr}{dt}=1/3\).
14
MediumCircle
The area of a circle is increasing at \(8\pi\text{ cm}^2/\text{s}\). How fast is the radius increasing when \(r=4\)?
Answer: C) \(1\) cm/s
\(A=\pi r^2\Rightarrow \frac{dA}{dt}=2\pi r\frac{dr}{dt}\).
At \(r=4\): \(8\pi=8\pi\frac{dr}{dt}\), so \(\frac{dr}{dt}=1\).
15
HardShadow
A 20-ft streetlight casts a shadow of a 5-ft person walking away from the light at \(4\) ft/s. How fast is the shadow length increasing?
Answer: B) \(4/3\) ft/s
Similar triangles: \(\dfrac{20}{x+s}=\dfrac{5}{s}\Rightarrow 20s=5x+5s\Rightarrow 15s=5x\Rightarrow s=x/3\).
Differentiate: \(\frac{ds}{dt}=\frac13\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac13(4)=\)\(4/3\) ft/s.

Implicit Differentiation

16
EasySlope
For \(x^2+y^2=25\), what is \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) at the point \((3,4)\)?
Answer: A) \(-3/4\)
Differentiate: \(2x+2y\frac{dy}{dx}=0\Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx}=-x/y\). At \((3,4)\), slope \(=-3/4\).
17
MediumFormula
If \(x^2y+y^3=10\), then \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\)
Answer: A) \(-\dfrac{2xy}{x^2+3y^2}\)
Differentiate: \(2xy+x^2\frac{dy}{dx}+3y^2\frac{dy}{dx}=0\).
\((x^2+3y^2)\frac{dy}{dx}=-2xy\), so \(\frac{dy}{dx}=-\dfrac{2xy}{x^2+3y^2}\).
18
MediumTangent Line
For the curve \(x^2+xy+y^2=7\), what is the slope of the tangent line at \((1,2)\)?
Answer: A) \(-4/5\)
\((x+2y)\frac{dy}{dx}=-(2x+y)\), so \(\frac{dy}{dx}=-\dfrac{2x+y}{x+2y}\).
At \((1,2)\): \(-\dfrac{4}{5}\).
19
HardSecond Derivative
For the circle \(x^2+y^2=25\), what is \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}\) at the point \((0,5)\)?
Answer: A) \(-1/5\)
For \(x^2+y^2=25\), one useful result is \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}=-\dfrac{25}{y^3}\). At \((0,5)\), this becomes \(-25/125=\)\(-1/5\).
20
HardHorizontal Tangents
For the curve \(x^2+xy+y^2=3\), which \(x\)-values give horizontal tangents?
Answer: B) \(x=\pm1\)
Horizontal tangents mean \(\frac{dy}{dx}=0\), so \(2x+y=0\Rightarrow y=-2x\). Substitute into the curve:
\(x^2-2x^2+4x^2=3\Rightarrow 3x^2=3\Rightarrow x=\pm1\).

Particle Motion

21
EasyVelocity
If \(s(t)=t^3-3t^2\), what is the velocity at \(t=1\)?
Answer: A) \(-3\)
\(v(t)=s'(t)=3t^2-6t\). At \(t=1\), \(v=3-6=\)\(-3\).
22
MediumDistance
A particle has velocity \(v(t)=t-2\) on \([0,5]\). What is the total distance traveled?
Answer: B) \(13/2\)
Velocity changes sign at \(t=2\). Distance \(=\int_0^2(2-t)\,dt+\int_2^5(t-2)\,dt=2+\frac92=\)\(\frac{13}{2}\).
23
HardSpeeding Up
A particle has position \(s(t)=t^3-3t^2-9t\). On which interval is the particle speeding up for \(t>0\)?
Answer: C) \((3,\infty)\)
\(v(t)=3t^2-6t-9=3(t-3)(t+1)\), so for \(t>0\), \(v<0\) on \((0,3)\) and \(v>0\) on \((3,\infty)\).
\(a(t)=6t-6\), so \(a>0\) for \(t>1\). Speeding up requires same sign. That happens on \((3,\infty)\).
24
MediumDirection Change
If \(v(t)=(t-1)(t-3)\), at what times does the particle change direction?
Answer: C) \(t=1\) and \(t=3\)
The sign of \(v\) is positive for \(t<1\), negative for \(13\). So the particle changes direction at both zeros.
25
EasyDisplacement
A particle has velocity \(v(t)=2t-2\) on \([0,3]\). What is its displacement?
Answer: C) 3
Displacement \(=\int_0^3(2t-2)\,dt=[t^2-2t]_0^3=9-6=\)3.

Min / Max / Concavity

26
MediumFirst Derivative Test
If \(f'(x)=(x-1)(x+2)\), where does \(f\) have a local maximum?
Answer: A) \(x=-2\)
\(f'\) changes from positive to negative at \(x=-2\), so \(f\) has a local maximum there. At \(x=1\), \(f'\) changes from negative to positive, so \(f\) has a local minimum there.
27
EasyConcavity
If \(f''(x)=x-3\), on what interval is \(f\) concave down?
Answer: A) \((-\infty,3)\)
Concave down means \(f''<0\). Since \(x-3<0\) when \(x<3\), the answer is \((-\infty,3)\).
28
MediumAbsolute Maximum
Find the absolute maximum value of \(f(x)=-x^2+6x+1\) on \([0,5]\).
Answer: B) 10
Vertex at \(x=-\frac{b}{2a}=3\). Then \(f(3)=-9+18+1=10\). Endpoints give \(f(0)=1\), \(f(5)=6\), so the absolute maximum is 10.
29
HardIncreasing / Decreasing
If \(f'(x)=x^2-4\), on which interval is \(f\) decreasing?
Answer: B) \((-2,2)\)
\(f\) is decreasing where \(f'<0\). Solve \(x^2-4<0\Rightarrow x^2<4\Rightarrow -2
30
HardInflection Point
If \(f''(x)=(x-2)^3\), at which \(x\)-value does \(f\) have an inflection point?
Answer: C) \(x=2\)
An inflection point occurs where \(f''\) changes sign. Since \((x-2)^3\) is negative for \(x<2\) and positive for \(x>2\), the sign changes at \(x=2\).
